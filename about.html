Our Story

Company Overview

Orion Pharma is an exporting organization specializing in products for human infertility, gynecology, oncology and critical care medicines, committed to providing high-quality medicines at affordable prices.

Established in 2017, Orion Pharma has built a trusted presence in the Indian healthcare market by maintaining consistent quality and reliability. Supported by a team of experienced professionals, we believe our people are our greatest strength and work continuously to meet the evolving needs of our customers.

We offer premium formulation brands sourced from approved and audited manufacturing partners that follow stringent quality control standards and operate state-of-the-art facilities.
