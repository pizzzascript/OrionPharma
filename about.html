Our Story

A Legacy of Innovation Since 2017

Founded in 2017, Orion Pharma began with a singular vision: to democratize high-quality healthcare across the Indian market. Our journey has been defined by a relentless pursuit of scientific excellence and an unwavering commitment to patient well-being.

From a small team of visionary researchers to a leading pharmaceutical powerhouse, we have consistently pushed the boundaries of what's possible in drug delivery and therapeutic efficacy.

We offer premium formulation brands sourced from approved and audited manufacturing partners that follow stringent quality control standards and operate state-of-the-art facilities.
