Women's Healthcare Solutions

Orion Pharma is committed to improving women's health and well-being by ensuring reliable access to high-quality gynecology and obstetrics formulations. Our product selection encompasses vital endocrine and hormonal support structures.

We supply specialized progesterone formulations, estrogen replacement therapies, and non-invasive delivery mechanisms (such as vaginal gels) sourced from GMP-certified pharmaceutical manufacturing facilities. Our focus is to deliver therapeutic consistency to healthcare networks, pharmacies, and clinics worldwide.

Key Treatment Focus Areas

  • ✓
    Luteal Phase Support: Formulations featuring micronized progesterone (capsules and vaginal gels) to prepare and maintain the uterine lining during conception.
  • ✓
    Pregnancy Maintenance: Hormonal support structures indicated for the prevention of threatened miscarriage or habitual abortion.
  • ✓
    Estrogen Replacement Therapies: Targeted estradiol tablet and transdermal gel variations to address hormonal deficiencies and manage menopause symptoms.
  • ✓
    Endocrine Regulation: Treatments to balance estrogen and progesterone ratios, assisting in the management of menstrual disorders and cycle irregularity.
