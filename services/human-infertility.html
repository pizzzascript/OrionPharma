Reproductive Health Solutions

At Orion Pharma, we specialize in exporting a comprehensive and highly advanced portfolio of medications dedicated to human infertility treatment. We understand that reproductive healthcare demands the highest degree of precision, efficacy, and consistency.

Through our partnerships with state-of-the-art, GMP-certified manufacturers, we secure and supply regulatory-compliant gonadotropins, hormone formulations, and recombinant therapies. Our supply chain ensures these temperature-sensitive materials maintain their integrity from production to delivery.

Key Treatment Focus Areas

  • ✓
    Controlled Ovarian Stimulation (COH): Facilitating multiple follicular development in IVF and ICSI cycles through purified and recombinant FSH (Follicle-Stimulating Hormone) and HMG (Human Menopausal Gonadotropin).
  • ✓
    Ovulation Induction & Triggering: Highly reliable recombinant and urinary hCG (Human Chorionic Gonadotropin) formulations to induce final oocyte maturation.
  • ✓
    Premature LH Surge Prevention: Supplying premium GnRH (Gonadotropin-Releasing Hormone) antagonists (like Cetrorelix and Ganirelix) to preserve cycle integrity.
  • ✓
    Male Infertility Management: Hormonal therapies designed to stimulate spermatogenesis in hypogonadotropic hypogonadal males.
