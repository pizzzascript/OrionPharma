Clinical Segment

Acute & Critical Care Solutions

In critical care medicine, safety, immediacy, and reliability are paramount. Orion Pharma exports a vital segment of hospital-grade acute therapies and anticoagulants designed to support emergency and critical patient management.

Our portfolio specializes in Low Molecular Weight Heparins (LMWH) and critical injectable anticoagulants such as Enoxaparin Sodium. Sourced from audited facilities following WHO-GMP guidelines, our products undergo strict inspection parameters to ensure that emergency rooms and clinical centers receive optimal formulations.

Key Treatment Focus Areas

  • ✓
    DVT Prophylaxis: Standard-of-care anticoagulant support to prevent Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT) in patients undergoing orthopedic or general surgery.
  • ✓
    Pulmonary Embolism (PE) Treatment: Rapid-response low molecular weight heparins for managing thromboembolic complications.
  • ✓
    Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS): Unfractionated and low molecular weight heparins indicated in managing unstable angina and myocardial infarction.
  • ✓
    Extracorporeal Circulation: Preventing clot formation inside hemodialysis lines and heart-lung bypass apparatus.
